Welding & NC3

From the first arc to all-position SMAW, TIG, and metal fab — certified through NC3, with the coupons on this page.

See the coupons → Full welding room

The problem

A weld is only good if it holds. I needed to go from striking an arc to test coupons that survive a bend, in more than the flat position.

What I did

Three SMAW classes at College of the Canyons — WELD-101A, 101B, and 101C — took me from flat-position stringer beads through horizontal, vertical, and overhead groove and fillet welds. I learned to choose electrodes and settings, control arc length and heat, find and correct defects, and prepare coupons for certification.

In TIG (WELD-112) I worked on torch control, adding filler rod, and keeping a clean weld on mild steel and aluminum. Metal fabrication (WELD-100) was blueprints, layout, cutting, and assembly with shop tools.

NC3 certified each level of that SMAW sequence: Intro to SMAW, Advanced SMAW, Principles of Welding, and Welding Safety. I also work as a student teaching assistant and lab technician for the welding and manufacturing department.
